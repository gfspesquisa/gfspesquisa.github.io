Unesp's Ilha Solteira campus (FEIS) released the video **"Cultive seu futuro na Engenharia Agronômica da Unesp Feis"** (*Grow your future in Agronomy at Unesp FEIS*), which introduces the program: how it is organized, career areas and opportunities during the degree.

I had the opportunity to contribute to promoting the program as **narrator and participant** in the video.
