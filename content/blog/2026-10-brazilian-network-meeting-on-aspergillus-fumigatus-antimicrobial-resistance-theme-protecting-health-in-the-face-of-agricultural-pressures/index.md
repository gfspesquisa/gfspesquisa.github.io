[Summa Phytopathologica v 51](https://repositorio.unesp.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/15b9c6f2-a225-406e-af25-f2c13328484a/content) special edition.

From December 3 to 5, 2025, the Faculty of Agronomic Sciences—Botucatu Campus—hosted the meeting titled “Meeting of the Brazilian Network on Antimicrobial Resistance in _Aspergillus fumigatus_: Theme: Protecting Health in the Face of Agricultural Pressures.” The conference addressed topics related to Brazilian and international legislation on pesticides, particularly the use of fungicides, and featured representatives from government and nongovernmental organizations in North, Central, and South America, as well as Europe.
