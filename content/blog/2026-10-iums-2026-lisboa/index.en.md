From **4 to 6 November 2026**, I'll be at **IUMS 2026**, the biennial congress of the International Union of Microbiological Societies, in **Lisbon, Portugal**. This year's theme is *Beyond the Microscope: AI & Innovation in Microbiology*.

I'll present two studies developed at FEIS/UNESP. Both look for alternatives to conventional fungicides against *Fusarium verticillioides*, a fungus that attacks maize and can produce mycotoxins:

1. **Dolomite–silver composite:** an abundant, low-cost mineral impregnated with silver, tested against the fungus in the laboratory.
2. **Red propolis from Alagoas:** a Brazilian natural product, evaluated at different concentrations with dose-response curves.

Behind-the-scenes updates will be on Instagram, and the abstracts will be added to the [Publications](/en/publications/) page after the event.

More about the congress: [iums2026.com](https://iums2026.com).
