Our study **"Nutritional characterization of spent *Pleurotus ostreatus* compost for use as a biofertilizer"** received an **honorable mention** in the first round of the **38th Unesp Undergraduate Research Congress (CIC)**. This round took place in Ilha Solteira (São Paulo, Brazil) on 23–24 September 2026. With the mention, the study **advanced to the second round** of the event.

This year's theme was *Unesp 50 Years: Science, Education, Innovation and Transformation in the Territories*.

![Title slide of the presentation at the 38th CIC Unesp](slide-titulo-cic.jpg "Title slide of the presentation (in Portuguese).")

## About the study

After oyster mushrooms are harvested, a large volume of growing substrate is left over: the spent compost. In this study, we analyzed its nutritional composition to assess its use as a **biofertilizer**, turning a residue into a source of nutrients for plants. Learn more on the [research project](/en/research/substrato-pleurotus/) page.

**Authors:** Gabriel Felix da Silva, Philippe Solano Toledo Silva and Ana Maria Rodrigues Cassiolato (FEIS/UNESP).

Thanks to my advisor, to my colleagues at the FEIS/UNESP Microbiology Laboratory and DEFERS, and to our partners Funghi & Flora and Cogumelos Coloniais.
