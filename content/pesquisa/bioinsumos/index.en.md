Bacteria such as ***Azospirillum*** and ***Bacillus*** help plants grow: they promote root
development, nutrient uptake and stress tolerance. They are the basis of many **bio-inputs**, which
reduce dependence on chemical inputs.

This project develops a microbial bio-input proposal and was submitted to the
**China International College Students' Innovation Competition (CICSIC 2026)**.
