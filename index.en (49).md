*Aspergillus fumigatus* is a common environmental fungus that can cause serious infections in people
with weakened immunity. Its resistance to **azole** antifungals is a public health concern, and part
of it is linked to the use of similar fungicides in agriculture — an example of how human health and
food production are connected (the **One Health** approach).

In this project, we investigate **red propolis** as a source of compounds active against resistant
isolates of *A. fumigatus*.

> Assays with *A. fumigatus* are carried out in the laboratory under biosafety protocols.
